# Design for Disassembly (DFD) Engineering Log

Welcome to my DFD engineering portfolio. Here I track virtual teardowns and analyze hardware through the lens of sustainability, recyclability, and disassembly design.

## 🛠️ My DFD Engineering Checklist
Whenever I analyze a device, I audit it against these four core principles:

1. **Fasteners & Hardware:** Minimum screw types, standard drives (Phillips), and reliance on snap-fits over hardware.
2. **Bonding & Materials:** Zero permanent glues, presence of thermal/mechanical release mechanisms, and clean material separation.
3. **High-Wear Component Access:** First-layer access for batteries, ports, and screens without pulling fragile motherboards.
4. **Modular Architecture:** Independent sub-assemblies and plug-in connectors instead of hand-soldered wires.
