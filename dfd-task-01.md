# DFD Study Log 01: Virtual Teardown Analysis

## 1. Target Device
* **Device Name:** iPhone 14 Pro
* **iFixit Guide Link/Title:** iPhone 14 Pro Battery Replacement

## 2. Disassembly Assessment
* **Enclosure Fastening Method:** Heavy waterproof adhesive around the screen + proprietary Pentalobe and Tri-point screws internally.
* **Battery Accessibility:** Moderate to Difficult. Protected by a metal shield, multi-tool screws, and stretch-release adhesive tabs that can easily snap and trap the battery.

## 3. Engineering Critique
* **The Main Obstacle:** The reliance on heat-softened adhesive to seal the display and chassis, plus using non-standard screw heads (Tri-point) instead of a universal Phillips head.
* **My DFD Redesign Recommendation:** Replace the perimeter display glue with a reusable mechanical rubber gasket and snap-fit clips, and use standard Phillips screws throughout the entire internal housing so only one screwdriver is needed.

## 4. Business vs. Sustainability Context
* **Why Manufacturers Do This:** To encourage planned obsolescence. Making battery replacement difficult pushes consumers to buy a new device rather than repairing the old one, increasing company sales at the expense of environment and recyclability.
